### Hairstyle

**Half-up, half-down using ACC-002** is the favorite. Gently collect the upper half of her hair and secure with the ivory lace bunny-ear scrunchie, leaving the remainder loose. Alternatives: relaxed side braid with ACC-014, or a comfortable high ponytail with ACC-002. Keep any ties comfortable and glasses unobstructed.
